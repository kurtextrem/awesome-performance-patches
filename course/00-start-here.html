Make it fast: a practical performance course

A quick, self-paced intro to the findings in this list. Proven fixes from open-source projects such as pnpm, React, TypeScript and Vue, taken from the links in awesome-performance-patches and grouped the way that list groups them.

Each lesson teaches 2 or 3 ideas, each with a small worked example and a diagram.

Where the big wins come from

Fixes fall into four levels. Work from the top, and stop when it is fast enough.

  1. Skip it, do it once, index it. Lessons 04 to 09. One pnpm step went from 35 seconds to 0.2.
  2. Stop waiting in line. Lessons 10, 11 and 24. Fewer round trips.
  3. Hold less memory. Lessons 12 to 15.
  4. Help the engine. Lessons 27 and 28. Usually 10 to 20 percent, so do this last.
